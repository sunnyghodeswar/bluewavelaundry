Sneaker cleaning that rides along with laundry

Shoe wash at Blue Wave is a per-pair service, not a kilo add-on dumped in with shirts. Sneakers, leather shoes, sandals and boots are cleaned and deodorised, then returned with your laundry order in Hinjewadi, Wakad or Baner.

Start at ₹199 a pair for canvas or mesh sneakers. Leather, sandals and boots are listed on the homepage rate card. Pickup is free when the combined order is above ₹300.

What we can and cannot promise

Fresh mud, dust and everyday grey come out well. Dye-transfer, deep oxidation on old white rubber, and broken glue are not a magic restore. We look at the pair at the door and tell you if cleaning is worth it.
