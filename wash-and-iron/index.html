Pressed laundry, not a rumpled stack

Wash and iron is for clothes that have to be wearable the hour they arrive. We wash the load on its own cycle, steam-press, and return pieces on hangers. The rate is ₹109 per kg in Hinjewadi, Wakad and Baner.

Pickup is free above ₹300. If you want this every week without rebooking, the household monthly plan on the homepage bills wash & iron by the month.

What we press

  • Office shirts and trousers
  • Kurtas, kurtis, casual dresses
  • School uniforms

Heavily embroidered, silk or structured garments are safer on dry cleaning. We will say so at the door rather than steam-press something we should not.
